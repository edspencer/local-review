---
title: Follow-on stacks
description: Park work for later without taking it out of review.
---

A stack marked `follow_on: true` is **parked work**: PRs to get to later, reviewed like any other but held back from the stacks going up now. They are always yellow, sit at the end of a repo, and are left out of every total.
